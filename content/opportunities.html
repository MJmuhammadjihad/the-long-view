07 / Opportunity watchlist

Where the overlooked work is.

Use these as a watchlist of problems to investigate. Demand growth, personal fit, accessible jobs and investment returns are separate questions; no field here is established as your future winner.

Do not confuse a bottleneck with your bargaining power.

Even when a problem is important, the customer, employer, platform, distributor or owner may capture most of the value. “Boring work” can remain underpaid. You need a reachable payer, credible delivery and economics that leave something for you.

How this watchlist was selected: capital requirements, transferability, accessible entry routes, and downside if the thesis is wrong. The selection is a judgment with a technical and Bangladesh emphasis. Confidence labels are qualitative judgments, not probabilities. Timelines describe possible learning or proof milestones, not guaranteed time to employment.

7 opportunities · hypotheses to investigate, not promised returns

Hypothesis A · test now

Business software & workflow integration

01 / 07

The unglamorous work: spreadsheet imports, permissions, duplicate records, order status, invoices, integrations, failed jobs and support.

CapitalLow capital
Planning estimateExample study window: 4–8 weeks
JudgmentPersonal fit untested

Who pays, and why

A distributor, service company, agency or small manufacturer can pay when you reduce errors, shorten a reporting cycle or improve visibility. The budget owner may be the proprietor or operations manager.

Why it may matter more

As software becomes easier to produce, understanding requirements and making a system work inside a real organization may become more important. This is a reasoned hypothesis, not proof that integration fees will rise.[02][06][07]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Learn Python, SQL, Git, HTTP, basic web interfaces, one backend framework, tests and deployment. Start with a developer internship, implementation role or supervised agency work.

A small test

Build an order-and-payment tracker with CSV import, input validation, access controls, an audit trail, export and a tested backup restore. Demonstrate it with synthetic data to five operators.

What could make this a bad bet

A simple spreadsheet or existing ERP may already solve the problem. Customers can be price-sensitive; custom software can create unlimited support work. If conversations reveal no funded problem, review whom you approached, the problem and the offer. A small conversation count is a diagnostic prompt, not proof of no market.

An advantage worth building

Specialize in one workflow, deliver reliably, and retain reusable components you have the right to own. Document results and earn referrals. Do not rely on secret prompts as your advantage.

Hypothesis B · test now

Quality assurance & technical support

02 / 07

Reproducing bugs, checking edge cases, documenting incidents, testing APIs, supporting users and verifying that a release did not break something.

CapitalLow capital
Planning estimateExample study window: 3–6 weeks
JudgmentPersonal fit untested

Who pays, and why

Software teams buy fewer defects, faster issue diagnosis and dependable support. The hiring manager or engineering lead is the buyer.

Why it may matter more

More software can mean more integration and verification work, but automated testing tools also improve. Pure repetitive manual checking is a vulnerable specialization.[02][06]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Learn browser tools, HTTP, SQL, test design and clear bug reports. Add one automation framework after you understand manual tests. Apply to trainee QA, application support and implementation support roles.

A small test

Publish a test plan and 10 reproducible bug reports on a project you own or are permitted to test; automate five meaningful regression cases and explain the failures.

What could make this a bad bet

Entry roles can be crowded or low paid. A “QA” title does not guarantee mentorship. Avoid jobs that are unpaid production labour with an indefinite promise of conversion.

An advantage worth building

Develop product understanding, root-cause analysis, testing judgment and customer communication. Progress into automation, reliability or product engineering.

Add after fundamentals

Data quality, reporting & applied AI evaluation

03 / 07

Cleaning inconsistent records, reconciling totals, documenting definitions, checking model output, tracing sources and identifying failures.

CapitalLow to moderate
Planning estimateUseful foundation: 3–9 months
JudgmentPlausible thesis · local demand unverified

Who pays, and why

An operations or finance team pays for trustworthy reports; an AI product team pays for evaluations that expose costly errors. The value is decision quality and avoided rework.

Why it may matter more

Cheaper generation can increase demand for trustworthy inputs and verification. Yet businesses may underfund quality, and many evaluation tasks will also be automated.[01][03][04]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Build strong SQL and Python first. Learn basic statistics, sampling, precision/recall, costs of false positives, and access control. Add AI APIs only when a conventional baseline exists.

A small test

Create a report pipeline for messy sales CSVs. Reject invalid records, reconcile totals, show data lineage, and measure errors before and after. Later add a document-extraction experiment with a held-out test set.

What could make this a bad bet

Do not call a dashboard “data engineering” or a prompt test “AI safety expertise.” Real reliability roles need deeper systems knowledge. Reject projects whose sensitive data you cannot handle safely.

An advantage worth building

Become the person who can explain what a number means, where it came from and when it should not be trusted. Combine that with one industry’s vocabulary.

A later specialization

Security, identity & operational reliability

04 / 07

Account permissions, backups, patching, logs, recovery, incident response and documenting who can access what.

CapitalLow lab cost; high trust barrier
Planning estimateTypically a longer apprenticeship
JudgmentMedium thesis; low six-month fit

Who pays, and why

Organizations pay to limit downtime and losses, meet contractual obligations and keep customer trust. Demand depends on actual budgets and perceived risk.

Why it may matter more

More digital dependence creates more systems to protect. It does not make a beginner qualified to lead security work, nor guarantee a shortage in every entry-level role.[23]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Start through software, support or systems work. Learn networking, Linux, authentication, authorization, secure development and recovery. Practise only on owned systems or explicitly authorized labs.

A small test

Implement role-based access, secret handling, dependency updates and a successful backup restore in your portfolio app. Write a short threat model and a realistic recovery checklist.

What could make this a bad bet

Certifications alone can become an expensive detour. Security audits and production access carry serious responsibility. Do not sell expertise you do not yet have.

An advantage worth building

Earn trust through supervised experience, clear incident analysis and a record of making systems recoverable. Later choose a narrow specialty.

A future domain to investigate

Cooling, energy & industrial maintenance software

05 / 07

Maintenance schedules, equipment histories, spare-parts tracking, energy records and documenting recurring faults.

CapitalLow for software; high for hardware
Planning estimateDomain learning: 6–24+ months
JudgmentStrong need; uncertain business fit

Who pays, and why

Facilities, factories, maintenance firms and equipment distributors can pay when reduced downtime or energy waste exceeds the service cost. Procurement access is often the hard part.

Why it may matter more

IEA evidence points to growing electricity work and skill bottlenecks. Bangladesh-specific heat evidence supports investigating cooling and efficiency. These trends do not establish that your proposed app has a market.[08][09][10]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Use your software background to work alongside qualified technicians or an existing service company. Learn maintenance operations and basic energy economics. Hands-on electrical or refrigeration work requires appropriate training and supervision.

A small test

Interview five maintenance teams; make an equipment register and recurring-work tracker using sample data. Ask whether an existing tool would solve their problem before building anything custom.

What could make this a bad bet

Long sales cycles, unreliable data, procurement barriers, liability and existing industrial systems can defeat a small newcomer. Do not buy equipment or finance installations to test the thesis.

An advantage worth building

A later advantage could combine trustworthy software with relationships and practical knowledge of one kind of facility. Treat it as a domain option, not your immediate income plan.

Another long-term domain

Care operations & accessible services

06 / 07

Scheduling, transport coordination, caregiver availability, accessible interfaces and administrative follow-up.

CapitalVariable; credential-dependent
Planning estimateValidate locally before committing
JudgmentStrong need; weak pay inference

Who pays, and why

Care providers, employers or families may pay for reliable coordination. Affordability, trust, regulation and responsibility can limit both price and margins.

Why it may matter more

Population ageing is a durable global trend, but increased care need does not guarantee high wages. Care work can remain poorly paid despite social importance.[11]

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

For you, investigate administrative software with an established provider. Clinical care requires relevant qualifications; a CS background is not permission to provide medical advice.

A small test

Map one nonclinical workflow with a provider and prototype scheduling with synthetic records. Test whether staff can use it and who would pay.

What could make this a bad bet

Sensitive data, safeguarding, liability and weak budgets are substantial barriers. Avoid a speculative marketplace that needs both providers and customers before it works.

An advantage worth building

Trust, dependable service and integration with actual care operations matter more than a generic app. Keep this outside the first six-month sprint.

Watch for a specific buyer

Agriculture, logistics & water operations

07 / 07

Stock loss, delivery reconciliation, equipment maintenance, water-use records, crop procurement and cold-chain handoffs.

CapitalLow for analysis; often high in assets
Planning estimateLonger local discovery
JudgmentPlausible; not validated here

Who pays, and why

An aggregator, processor, distributor or established operator may pay for a specific reduction in waste or delays. A small farmer’s important problem may still lack an affordable solution.

Why it may matter more

Food, water and distribution remain essential, but that alone does not establish scarcity rents. Local regulation, weather, working capital and fragmented buyers shape the economics.

Explore the entry path, experiment, and reasons to walk away

Your route in

Begin with interviews and process mapping. Your entry is a reporting or operations role, not buying land, vehicles or cold storage with borrowed money.

A small test

Use sample transaction data to reconcile purchased, delivered, rejected and unpaid quantities. Compare the result with the operator’s current spreadsheet.

What could make this a bad bet

No local willingness-to-pay study was completed for this guide. Drop the idea if you cannot access records, reach the payer or show an improvement worth buying.

An advantage worth building

Investigate only after your core software skills and income are established, or when a trusted contact offers supervised paid work.
