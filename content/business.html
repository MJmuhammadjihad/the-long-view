05 / Jobs & first clients

Find a payer before building a company.

Employment is a valid first business decision. A small service is an alternative to test when you have the competence and time; an agency or startup is not the default answer to needing income quickly.

Choose one primary earning channel

If you are still developing practical skills, a legitimate paid role with good supervision deserves serious consideration. Compare it with a small service only when you can responsibly deliver that service. You are not expected to run a full-time job, full degree load and freelance business simultaneously. Count the hours first.

Channel A · Employment

Target internships, trainee/junior backend roles, implementation support, application support and QA roles with real supervision. Use university alumni, faculty introductions, employer career pages and reputable job boards.

Ask what you will learn, who reviews your work, whether the role is paid, the working hours, the contract terms and how conversion decisions are made. Do not equate “internship to full-time” with a guaranteed offer.

Channel B · A narrow service

Use local introductions and small agencies to reach operators with a recurring problem. Offer data cleanup, a report workflow, an integration repair or a bounded internal tool you can support.

Start with a specific output and acceptance test. A supervised subcontract can be more realistic than finding a high-paying foreign client alone from day one.

Two employer pages checked for this guide illustrate relevant requirements: AppifyDevs describes backend language fundamentals, SQL/APIs, testing and critical AI review; ZOOM IT describes business software and web development. These are small, time-sensitive snapshots—not a representative vacancy survey or evidence that you will earn ৳50,000. Recheck openings and independently verify employers.[06][07]

Discovery before development

  1. Ask for the last real example. “Show me how this report was made last week.” Observe actual work rather than imagined needs.
  2. Estimate the cost. Time spent, errors, delayed collections, lost sales or missed deadlines. Separate measured amounts from guesses.
  3. Find the decision-maker. Who owns the budget? Who would use the solution? Who can give access? They may be different people.
  4. Check existing alternatives. A training session, simpler spreadsheet or existing tool may be the best solution.
  5. Offer a small paid test. Agree a narrow outcome, duration, maximum cost and the evidence needed to continue.

An honest first message

“I’m [your role and location], building [a service you can actually deliver]. I’m trying to understand where your team loses time with spreadsheets. Could I see a nonconfidential example of the workflow? If there’s a clear fit, I can propose a small, fixed-scope pilot.”

Adapt this to the person and your actual ability. Do not claim experience, customers or savings you do not have. This is a template for you to use; no messages have been sent.

Scope and protect the economics

A simple proposal should name the problem, deliverables, exclusions, data access, milestones, acceptance test, price, revision limit, payment dates, ownership and support period. Use a deposit or staged payments where appropriate. Do not fund a large client project from money the household needs.

Effective hourly earnings = (cash collected − direct costs − tax provision) ÷ all delivery, sales, meeting and support hours

An illustrative ৳12,000 project that costs ৳2,000 and takes 40 total hours leaves ৳250 per hour before tax provision. At 80 hours, that falls to ৳125. This is arithmetic, not a market rate. Scope and repeatability can matter as much as the headline price.

Stress cases before target arithmetic

If there is no offer by month four, expenses still fall due. If a client pays a month late, an invoice cannot fund that month’s groceries. A ৳20,000 offer may improve your position while leaving a large gap. Use the six-month cash planner with zero and lower income before considering the target combinations below.

Bangladesh-specific payment and relocation checks

Before taking an overseas project, verify that your bank and the payment service support your residency, service type and actual receiving route. Keep contracts, invoices, platform statements and remittance records. Bangladesh Bank’s July 2026 consolidated circular includes a framework for individual service exporters and electronic evidence; ask an authorized-dealer bank about current amendments and required records.[12]

Verify tax registration, filing obligations and any applicable exemption with current NBR guidance or a qualified local adviser. Do not assume foreign income, software work or freelancing is automatically tax-free.[18]

For readers already in Dhaka, local employers, classmates and business operators can be useful sources of feedback. Bangladesh’s industrial employment is concentrated in Dhaka and Chattogram, but that broad fact does not prove a job is available to you.[05] Move within Bangladesh when a concrete opportunity improves your net income, learning or living conditions after relocation costs.

For moving abroad, compare after-tax income minus rent, insurance, transport, visa costs and family support. Obtain a legitimate offer or funded study route, confirm work rights and credential recognition, and budget transition time. Avoid borrowing heavily for a migration agent’s promise. A remote portfolio and a recognized degree can improve options before a move.[13]

When a service deserves to become a product

Consider productization after several independent customers pay for substantially the same problem, use the solution repeatedly and accept similar workflows. Keep rights to your own reusable code, but respect client ownership and confidentiality. Test support workload, retention and gross margin before hiring or adding subscriptions.

You do not need to own everything immediately. Employment can fund learning and savings. Later, a maintenance practice, specialist service, software product or equity stake may let your income depend less entirely on hours. Ownership increases upside and risk; it is not a requirement for basic financial security.

Before accepting an offer or a pilot

For a job

Verify the employer, written salary, payment dates, probation, hours, leave, location, equipment, supervision and notice terms. Calculate net cash after travel or relocation. Ask whether you can continue university and whether outside work is permitted. Never pay a recruiter to unlock a guaranteed job.

For a first client

Confirm that you can complete a representative task before promising the whole job. Use authorized, minimized or synthetic data while learning. Set a time and cost limit, acceptance test and handover. Avoid safety-critical or sensitive production work you cannot review adequately.

Income arithmetic is not a route to demand. The combinations above show what must add up, not how likely those contracts or salaries are. If an interview pipeline or service offer is not converting, use the roadmap’s diagnosis table rather than assuming more hours alone will solve it.
