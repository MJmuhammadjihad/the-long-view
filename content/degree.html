THE LONG VIEW / PUBLIC TOOLKIT

Keep the door open. Price it honestly.

Do not finish automatically and do not quit impulsively. Price the remaining commitment against real, affordable alternatives.

The return on a credential is a question to investigate.

Completed credits can reduce the future cost of a useful qualification. They do not, by themselves, justify the remaining expense. Compare recognition, completion probability, affordability and a concrete alternative.

Decision tool / degree costs

What would finishing need to earn back?

Other costs begin at zero as a placeholder. Add fees, retakes, travel and relevant opportunity costs. Actual bills may arrive earlier than the average monthly provision. Simple payback omits discounting, job-search delay, noncompletion risk and nonfinancial benefits. A user-entered uplift is an assumption.

What the evidence can tell you

A recognized degree may help with screening, postgraduate admission and some migration routes. Germany has both qualification-based and experience-based IT routes, with additional conditions; this is an example of option value, not proof of eligibility.[13]

The US occupational handbook describes a degree as typical for software and QA roles. It does not price the return on an individual qualification in another country.[02] Bangladesh labour analysis describes education expanding faster than high-quality job creation. That challenges a degree-alone guarantee; it does not show that all degrees are worthless.[27]

What would change the decision?

Unaffordable debt, threatened essentials, poor recognition, low completion probability, a materially cheaper recognized route, or a concrete paid opportunity can change the balance. So can financial aid or a strong supervised pathway attached to study. Revisit the decision when those facts change.
