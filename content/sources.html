Inspect the basis of the advice

This is a targeted, AI-assisted research synthesis, not a systematic review or a representative salary survey. It combines institutional research, official guidance, employer pages and learning resources. Recommendations are provisional judgments; a source link does not turn a forecast into a fact.

Research date: 9 November 2026. Original publication dates are retained. Job pages can expire; rules and product terms can change. The guide does not update itself. A research or salary correction is a useful contribution.
