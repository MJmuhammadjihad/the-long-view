The central idea

Build useful capability. Keep room to change your mind.

No secret skill, forgotten asset or future boom can guarantee financial security. Start with affordable experiments, test who pays, protect essential cash and invest sustainable surplus. The guide shows the reasoning—and where it could be wrong.
