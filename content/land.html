08 / Land & long-term assets

Revisit land with a better question.

The useful question is not “Which place will boom?” It is “What can I buy at a sensible price, with a defensible thesis, without putting my family at risk?”

Three different decisions often called “buying land”

PurposeWhat to evaluateWhy the distinction matters
A home or family baseHousing utility, affordability, location, title, financing and alternatives to buying.It can be worthwhile as consumption and security even without exceptional investment returns.
An operating farmReliable yield, water, labour, inputs, tenant/operator skill, market access and net cash flow.Fertile soil alone is not a profitable farming business.
Speculative appreciationPurchase price, credible demand, holding costs, rights, liquidity and downside scenarios.A valuable future location can still be a bad investment at today’s asking price.

A property recommendation needs adequate capital, local information and an individual suitability assessment. Study how businesses and properties earn cash; revisit ownership after your household has a stable base and a defensible deal exists.

Land can create wealth. The purchase still needs to work.

A parcel can appreciate when jobs, transport, utilities and purchasing power grow around it. Farmland can generate operating or rental income. But quoted prices are not always realizable sale prices, and land’s scarcity does not make every location desirable. Title, access, soil, water, flood exposure and permitted use can matter more than acreage.

For you, there is an additional issue: even a successful land investment may not pay the bills during the years you wait. Capital tied up in land cannot simultaneously finance education, a family emergency or your first business. Illiquidity is especially costly before income is stable.

Put a “missed fortune” into annual terms

If a parcel rose from ৳100,000 to ৳2,000,000 over 30 years, the price multiplied 20 times, equivalent to roughly 10.5% annual nominal price growth. This is a hypothetical example, not a Bangladesh land-return estimate.

To evaluate the actual investment, include transaction costs, taxes, maintenance, any rent or crop income, financing, selling costs and inflation. Compare with feasible alternatives from that time. A large final number alone does not settle whether the original decision was sensible.

The property-level checklist

GateEvidence to obtain
Ownership and legal statusIndependent local lawyer checks the chain of title, relevant records, boundaries, encumbrances, inheritance/co-owner claims, disputes and the seller’s authority. A document or mutation entry alone is not a substitute for a complete legal review.
Physical access and useIndependent survey and site inspection; lawful road access; actual utilities; current zoning, development permissions and restrictions checked with the relevant authorities.
Environmental and farming conditionsFlood history, drainage, elevation, erosion or salinity where relevant, reliable water access, soil tests and operating feasibility. Inspect in more than one season where possible.
Demand and catalystsActual occupancy, job creation, reachable buyers or tenants, completed infrastructure and funded projects. Separate signed, funded works from rumours and promotional maps.
Valuation and cash flowComparable completed transactions where available, realistic rent or agricultural margins, all acquisition costs and a conservative sale or operating scenario.
Ownership and liquidity riskWho manages it? How long might a sale take? What happens if a co-owner refuses to sell, a tenant leaves, or development is delayed?
Household resilienceYou can fund essentials and reserves without selling this asset, and can withstand a long holding period with little or no appreciation.

Three ways to reduce the chance of an expensive mistake

  • Acquire operating knowledge before acreage. Work with a legitimate operator, study real accounts and understand maintenance and customer demand. An idea becomes more credible when it survives ordinary costs.
  • Use conservative cases. Test delayed infrastructure, lower rent, weak crop margins, flood damage, legal delay and a difficult resale. If the investment only works with optimistic appreciation, it is speculation.
  • Compare with doing nothing for now. Keeping liquidity while your income and knowledge improve is an active choice. Missing one rising parcel does not mean missing your only route to wealth.

Why picking the winner is not your only route

Historical research on US stocks shows that aggregate wealth creation was concentrated in a small share of firms. That illustrates the difficulty of identifying winners in advance and supports learning about diversification. It is not evidence about land returns in Bangladesh, nor a prediction of future stock performance.[16]

You can participate in future economic growth through skills, employment, a useful business and diversified investments without correctly identifying a single future boom town. Your aim is enough successful compounding, not ownership of every asset that later rises.

Do not buy a story about future scarcity until you understand today’s price, rights, cash flows and downside.
