# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-10

First public release of The Long View.

- Twelve chapters combine career experiments, education choices, cash planning, business fundamentals and long-term ownership.
- Five calculators model degree costs, weekly capacity, six-month cash flow, household reserves and savings sensitivity.
- Editable income scenarios include a zero-income case. Currency selection changes units without applying exchange rates or changing cited evidence.
- Thirty-one dated references separate observations, forecasts, advertisements and judgments.
- A local decision journal supports text export, validated JSON backups and explicit restore confirmation.
- Responsive light design, chapter search, keyboard navigation, print support and a standalone HTML download.
- Reproducible builds, automated functional checks and contribution templates.

The material is educational and AI-assisted. Functional and structural checks are automated; full cross-browser visual verification has not been completed. The cover is an original illustration, not an application screenshot. Research snapshot: 9 November 2026.
