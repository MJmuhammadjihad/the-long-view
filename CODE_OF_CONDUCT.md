# Community expectations

The Long View concerns decisions that can affect people’s livelihoods. Discussions should be candid, respectful, and grounded in evidence.

We welcome contributors with different countries, incomes, education, abilities, and levels of experience. Critique claims and implementations without shaming someone’s finances or background. Explain disagreement, make room for uncertainty, and accept corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deceptive promotion, spam, and fabricated evidence are not welcome. Do not pressure people to share personal financial or identity details.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed to protect the project and its participants. Apply these measures proportionately and without retaliation for good-faith reports.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the options on [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/MJmuhammadjihad), or use GitHub’s reporting tools when appropriate. Avoid posting sensitive details publicly. No response-time guarantee is offered.
